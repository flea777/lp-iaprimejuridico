# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Brazilian law-firm owners and legal professionals who want a more predictable client-acquisition operation and are evaluating a specialist partner for paid media, automation, CRM, and commercial enablement.

## Product Purpose

Present Prime Jurídico as a credible specialist for legal-sector growth, explain its integrated service offering, demonstrate real campaign outcomes, and move qualified visitors toward a direct commercial conversation.

## Brand Personality

Confident, precise, and sophisticated. The experience should feel technically capable and commercially grounded without becoming cold, aggressive, or theatrical.

## Anti-references

- A generic SaaS landing-page template disconnected from the legal market.
- Flashy effects, excessive motion, or ornamental luxury cues that weaken credibility.
- A drastic redesign that replaces the established graphite-and-gold identity or current page structure.
- Manipulative urgency, exaggerated promises, or interaction patterns that obscure the primary conversion path.

## Design Principles

- Preserve recognition while improving craft: refine the existing identity instead of replacing it.
- Make expertise legible: hierarchy and spacing should make the integrated offer easy to understand.
- Let evidence carry persuasion: results and concrete capabilities should feel more prominent than decoration.
- Keep conversion paths direct: every primary action should clearly lead toward a commercial conversation.
- Use restraint as proof of confidence: motion, depth, and accent color should support focus rather than compete for it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for text contrast, keyboard navigation, focus visibility, and semantic structure. Interactive targets should remain comfortable on touch devices, and motion must respect reduced-motion preferences.
